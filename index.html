06 / RESEARCH

BANGLA+ ENGLISH+ MATHEMATICS
🔬 Ongoing Research

Handwritten Expression Recognition

Existing OCR systems struggle to accurately recognize and reconstruct handwritten Bangla text and mathematical expressions appearing together in educational documents — especially into a structured, editable format such as LaTeX.

This research explores multimodal document understanding for the specific combination of Bangla script, English text, and mathematical notation — inspired by work such as UniRec-0.1B: Unified Text and Formula Recognition.

✦ Ongoing exploration. No fabricated results or publications.
